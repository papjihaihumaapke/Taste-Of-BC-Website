Good to know

Before you go.

  • Cashless site. Tap to pay at every booth.
  • Bring a refillable bottle. Free water stations throughout.
  • Bring ID. Needed for all alcohol tastings, no exceptions.
  • Dogs. Service animals only, please.
  • Rain or shine. Covered tents across the site.
