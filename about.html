About Taste of BC

A celebration of BC’s food community, open to everyone.

Taste of BC brings the province’s restaurants, makers and drink producers together on one field in Surrey — with free entry, so anyone can come and taste.

Our story

Free to enter. Fair for vendors. Easy for everyone.

We built the festival around one simple idea: remove the barriers. No admission fee keeps it open to families and newcomers. The $1 coupon system keeps booths fast, cashless and fair. And a single look — red arches, matching booth banners, one booklet — makes it easy to find your way.

Community first

Free entry so every neighbour in Surrey and beyond can join in.

Local makers

BC restaurants, bakers, brewers and winemakers — big names and first-timers.

Warm welcome

Heated seating, lights, music and a red-lanyard crew ready to help.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is entry really free?

Yes. Entry is free on both days at every entrance arch. You only pay for food and drinks, using festival coupons.

How do coupons work?

A coupon sheet costs $20 and contains 20 tear-off $1 coupons. Every menu is priced in coupons. Learn more.

Can I bring cash?

Taste of BC is cashless. Coupon Booths accept debit, credit, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You can also buy coupons online before you arrive.

Are there vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or halal options?

Yes — use the dietary filters on Restaurants & Menus. Labels are supplied by vendors; please ask at the booth about allergens.

Is the festival family-friendly?

Absolutely. There’s a Kids Zone and family seating. The Beer & Wine Garden is 19+ with ID.

What if it rains or snows?

The festival goes ahead in most weather. The Heated Dining Tent is open all day. In the rare case of a weather closure we’ll post updates here and on social media.

Where do I pick up merch or online coupon orders?

Coupon orders: any Coupon Booth at the gates. Merch orders: the Info Booth by the North Gate. Bring your order QR code.
