Getting there

Getting to Surrey.

Transit

Take SkyTrain and connecting buses toward the venue. Use the TransLink trip planner for the fastest route.

Driving & parking

On-site and nearby parking available. Lots fill up after 5 PM — carpool if you can.

Rideshare & taxi

Drop-off and pick-up zone at the North Gate (main entrance).

Accessible drop-off

Dedicated accessible drop-off at the West Gate, with step-free access onto the field.

Accessibility

A festival for everyone.

We’re planning the site so everyone can get around comfortably. Questions or requests? Contact us before the event.

  • Step-free main walkwaysMatting on the main routes between gates, booths and seating.
  • Accessible washroomsAt both washroom locations on the map.
  • Accessible seatingReserved spaces in the Heated Dining Tent and at the Main Stage.
  • Service animals welcomePets are not permitted on site.
  • Quiet spaceAsk at the Info Booth for the quiet area.

Dress for December

It’s an outdoor winter festival. Bring layers, a warm jacket and waterproof shoes. The Heated Dining Tent is open all day.

Good to know

  • Beer & Wine Garden is 19+ — bring government ID
  • No outside alcohol
  • Smoke- and vape-free site
  • Lost and found at the Info Booth
