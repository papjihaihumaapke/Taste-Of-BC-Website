Coupon FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need a ticket to get in?

No. Entry is free for everyone, both days. You only need coupons to buy food and drinks.

Can I pay a booth with cash or card directly?

No — booths only accept Taste of BC coupons. Buy coupons online or at a Coupon Booth (card and tap only) and you’re set.

What happens to coupons I don’t use?

Coupons are valid on both festival days. They have no cash value and can’t be exchanged for money after the event. See our refund policy.

I lost my sheet. Can it be replaced?

Coupons work like cash, so lost or stolen coupons can’t be replaced. Keep your sheet in a pocket or the free festival booklet sleeve.

Who are the logos on the back?

Our sponsors. Their support keeps entry free. Want your brand on the back of every sheet?
