Privacy policy

Taste of BC (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

  • Order details for coupon sheets and merchandise.
  • Basic, anonymised website analytics.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. See Stripe’s privacy policy for details.

How we use information
